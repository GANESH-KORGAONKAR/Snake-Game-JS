Snake Game

Ready to slither? Eat food, grow long, and set a high score!

Welcome to the retro arcade experience. You control a hungry snake in a confined grid. Your mission is simple: consume as much food as possible without crashing.

How to Play

Up Arrow / W / 8 : Move Up

Down Arrow / S / 2 : Move Down

Left Arrow / A / 4 : Move Left

Right Arrow / D / 6 : Move Right

Rules

Eat to Grow: Each apple increases your length and score.

Avoid Walls: Hitting the boundary ends the game.

Don't Bite Yourself: Colliding with your own tail is fatal.

No U-Turns: You cannot reverse directly into your own body.
